I am a computer engineering student at UC Irvine, working where control theory
meets embedded hardware. In the Resilient Cyber-Physical Systems Lab I build LLM
agents that turn natural-language commands into temporal-logic specifications
for quadcopter control, and on the ZotSun solar car team I build the vehicle's
telemetry system.
